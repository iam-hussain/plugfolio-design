What we don't ask you for.

These aren't settings or a plan tier. They're rules the product is built around.

No account, ever, to buy

No wall, no modal, no "sign up to continue" between you and the shop. An account exists only if you want to follow a creator or leave a comment.

No cart and no checkout

Plugfolio doesn't sell you anything. There's nothing to add up, so there's no total and no basket.

We never see your card

Payment happens on the retailer's site, under their terms. Your card details never touch us because we're never in that step.

Nothing is estimated

Every number a creator sees is directly measured. Where something can't be measured, we say so instead of guessing.

What we can and can't see.

A creator earns from these links, so it's fair to know what gets counted. This is the full list.

You tapped out to a shop Tracked
You copied a discount code Tracked
Whether you actually bought anything Not tracked
An in-store code you used at a till Not tracked

The last two are the honest part. We could guess at them; other tools do. We'd rather a creator trust the numbers we do show.

Questions people actually ask.

Do I need an account to buy something?

No. Shopping never needs an account on Plugfolio — that's a rule, not a default you can lose. An account only exists so you can follow creators and leave comments as yourself.

Does it cost more than buying direct?

No. You land on the retailer's normal page at their normal price. If the creator has a discount code, you get it cheaper.

Does the creator earn from this?

Usually yes — most links are affiliate links, and some products are the creator's own. Products a creator sells themselves are marked, so you always know which is which.

Where do returns and delivery go?

To the retailer you bought from. Plugfolio isn't part of the sale, so it can't help with an order — the shop's own support can.

What happens to my data?

Taps are counted so creators know which post worked. If you never make an account, there's no name attached to it.
